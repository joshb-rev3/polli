## Our Commitment to Privacy

[LEGAL ENTITY NAME] ("**Polli**," "**we**," "**us**," or "**our**") operates the Polli mobile applications, website at [https://polli-app.com](https://polli-app.com), and related services (the "**Service**"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use and share it, and the choices available to you.

Polli is a peer-to-peer gifting platform: people start Pollis for others, friends give small fixed-amount gifts, and recipients claim funds through our payment partner, Stripe. Because of that model, we may receive information **about** a person before they ever create an account.

By using the Service, you acknowledge this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, do not use the Service. Our Terms of Service also govern use of the Service.

## Applicability

This Privacy Policy applies to information we collect:

- When you create an Account or use the Service as a Starter, Donor, or Recipient
- When someone else starts a Polli for you and provides your name and contact details
- Through our website, apps, emails, SMS, and customer support
- From payment, authentication, and infrastructure partners that help us operate the Service

It does **not** cover third-party websites or services that we do not control, even if linked from Polli.

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Information you provide directly (Account holders)

When you create an Account or use the Service, we may collect:

- **Identity and contact information** — name, email address, phone number
- **Profile information** — display name, avatar, and other details you choose to add
- **Authentication information** — when you sign in with Apple, Google, or Facebook, we receive a token and basic profile fields from those providers; we do not receive your password for those services
- **Payment-related information** — when you give a Gift or receive a payout (see Section 1.4; **Stripe**, not Polli, handles and stores full card/bank details and government ID used for verification)
- **User Content** — Polli story text, private messages to a Recipient (including optional voice recordings and any transcriptions), donation notes, anonymity preferences, and similar content you submit
- **Support communications** — messages you send to customer support

### 1.2 Information collected about a Recipient from another User

**Important:** A person can have a Polli started for them before they create an Account or interact with Polli themselves.

When a Starter creates a Polli for someone (the "**Recipient**"), we collect the Recipient’s:

- First and last name
- Email address and/or phone number (used to notify them of the Polli)

This information is provided **by the Starter about the Recipient**, not collected directly from the Recipient.

**Recipient rights:** If a Polli was started for you and you do not want a public page associated with your name — or you want information corrected or removed — you may request takedown or correction at any time using Section 12, whether or not you have an Account. See also Section 6 (Public Information).

### 1.3 Information collected automatically

When you use the Service, we may collect:

- **Device and usage information** — device type, operating system, app version, browser type, language, crash logs, and general usage analytics
- **Log and network information** — IP address, access times, pages/screens viewed, referring URLs
- **Approximate location** — derived from IP address, used primarily for security and fraud prevention
- **Identifiers** — device identifiers, session identifiers, and similar technical IDs

We do not currently collect precise GPS location for core product features. If that changes, we will request permission where required and update this Policy.

### 1.4 Information from third-party services

- **Stripe** — payment status, payout status, whether identity/bank verification ("KYC") has been completed, and connected-account status. We do **not** receive or store full card numbers, bank account numbers, Social Security numbers, or government ID images; those are collected and stored by Stripe under Stripe’s privacy policy.
- **Supabase** — our backend database and authentication provider, which processes data on our behalf.
- **Social login providers** — Apple, Google, and/or Facebook, as you choose.
- **Communications providers** — email/SMS vendors used to send transactional notices ([SMS/EMAIL PROVIDER — TBD]).
- **Analytics / crash reporting** — if enabled, providers that help us understand performance and reliability ([ANALYTICS PROVIDERS — TBD]).

### 1.5 Information from other sources

We may receive information from fraud-prevention tools, public sources, or partners as needed to verify Accounts, prevent abuse, or comply with law.

We share personal information only as described below:

- Comply with law, regulation, legal process, or governmental request

- Detect, prevent, or address fraud, security, or technical issues
- Protect the rights, property, or safety of Polli, Users, or the public
- Comply with unclaimed-property / escheatment obligations

### 3.4 Business transfers

If we are involved in a merger, acquisition, financing, reorganization, bankruptcy, or sale of assets, personal information may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to customary confidentiality protections.

### 3.5 With your direction

We may share information when you ask us to (for example, sharing a Polli link yourself).

We do not share full payment card numbers, bank account numbers, or government identification with anyone other than Stripe (or as required by law).

Certain Polli activity is designed to be social and visible:

Think carefully before posting stories or notes you would not want others to see. Recipient takedown requests are described in Section 1.2 and Section 12.

## 8. Children’s Privacy

The Service is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under **13** through direct Account registration. You must be **18+** to create an Account, give a Gift, or claim a payout (see Terms of Service).

**Important limitation:** because a Starter can submit another person’s name, email, and/or phone number, it is possible for a minor’s information to be submitted without our independently verifying age. If we learn we have collected personal information from or about a child under 13 in a manner inconsistent with COPPA or other applicable law, we will delete that information and, where applicable, the associated Polli page. Parents or guardians who believe a child’s information was submitted without appropriate consent should contact us using Section 12.
