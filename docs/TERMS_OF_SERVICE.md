## 3. Description of the Service

polli lets users ("**starters**") start a Polli for another person ("**recipients**")
to receive small, fixed-amount monetary gifts from other users ("**donors**")
in connection with an occasion or story shared publicly through the app.
Key mechanics, which may be updated from time to time:

- Gifts are a fixed, small amount per donor per Polli (currently $1.00
  to the recipient, described at checkout); this amount and any associated
  processing-fee structure may change and will be clearly disclosed at the
  time of the transaction.
- A Polli runs for a defined time period selected by the starter at
  creation and then closes.
- A recipient may claim gifts made in their name by creating an account and
  completing identity/bank verification through our payment processor,
  Stripe, in order to receive funds.
- Starters may include a private message (including an optional voice
  recording) visible only to the recipient, and a public story visible to
  anyone who can view the Polli page.

We are a facilitator of these gifts. We are not a party to, and do not
guarantee, any gift, Polli, or relationship between users.

## 5. Unclaimed Balances

Because a recipient is not required to have an account at the time gifts are
made in their name, it is possible for gifted funds to go unclaimed if a
recipient never completes payout verification.

### 5.1 Automatic refund to donors

If a recipient has **not completed payout verification and withdrawn the
funds gifted to them within 60 days of the Polli's closing date**, we
will automatically refund each individual gift to the original donor's
payment method, in the amount originally charged, less any amounts
non-refundable to us by our payment processor (if any).

[NOTE TO COUNSEL / NOTE TO ENGINEERING: 60 days is a placeholder the

### 5.2 Fallback — where a refund cannot be completed

If an individual refund cannot be completed for any reason (for example, an
expired or closed payment method, or the original transaction is outside our
payment processor's refund window), the corresponding amount will instead be
held on the donor's or recipient's behalf, as applicable, and administered in
accordance with applicable unclaimed property (escheatment) law, including
any required attempts to locate the rightful owner before such funds are, if
still unclaimed after the applicable statutory period, remitted to the
relevant state unclaimed property program.
